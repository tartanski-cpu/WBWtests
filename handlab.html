Hand fit

Fit the hands to your controllers, then copy the numbers back. Put the headset on and press Enter passthrough to see your real hands with the game's hands over them, or Enter VR. In the headset, point at the floating panel and pull the trigger; B brings the panel back in front of you; grip makes a fist, grip with the stick pushed forward points.
