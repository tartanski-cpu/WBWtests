Tool bench

The hand tools, on their own, to try before they go into the game: a piece of engine room with a small fire, a damaged plate, a rusty bulkhead and a paint tin.

Desktop: click the room to look about (Esc to get the mouse back), WASD to walk.

  • 1–9 take a tool, 0 or the same number again puts it away; the mouse wheel steps through them
  • Left button uses it, right button its secondary
  • Controller: LB / RB step, hold LB for the wheel (right stick picks), RT use, LT secondary

VR: reach behind your head over either shoulder and squeeze the grip: the ring of tools opens. Point at one and grip to take it; hold the grip to hold it, let go and it's back in the backpack. One in each hand. Trigger uses it, A / X its secondary.
